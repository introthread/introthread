## 01 What Introthread does for you

You meet people at events, meetings and conferences. A week later most of those contacts are forgotten. Introthread fixes that: it remembers who you met and where, both of you confirm the meeting, and you each privately say what you want next. If you both want the same thing, you are both told and a follow-up is ready to send.

| You get | Because |
|---|---|
| Nothing forgotten | Every meeting is saved with the place, date and your private note. |
| No awkward follow-ups | Answers stay private; only a match is revealed. |
| Real results | Matches come with a ready message and reminders, so meetings turn into opportunities. |
| Easy for the other person | They need no app and no account. |

## 02 Five steps to get started

1. **Create your free card.** Go to introthread.com and choose **Create your free card**. Sign up with Google, LinkedIn or email, add your name, job title, company and photo, and pick a card style.
2. **Share your card.** Show your QR code (Event mode is best at busy events), send your link (introthread.com/your-name) by WhatsApp or email, or tap an NFC card (Pro). People can save your details to their phone straight away.
3. **Save who you met.** Open **Tap**, enter their name and email or phone, choose where you met and add a private note. Select **Save**. They get a short email asking them to confirm.
4. **Answer "What's next?"** Once they confirm, you each choose Meet again, Introduce someone, Stay in touch or Not now. Answers stay sealed until both have replied.
5. **Follow up.** If you match, you both get a "You matched" email. Open the Thread, use the ready follow-up (Pro members can rewrite it with AI) and send it from your own email or WhatsApp.

## 03 Running an event or community? Use Rooms

1. Open **Rooms > Create a Room**, add the name, date, place or online link, and choose public or private.
2. Share the Room link or QR code on screens, badges and tables. Attendees join with one tap.
3. Every connection made at the event is tagged to your Room.
4. Watch the results live (connections, confirmations, meetings agreed, introductions) and export them for sponsors.

One event a month for up to 20 people is free. Bigger or extra events need a Room pass at $59 per event.

## 05 Tips

- Install Introthread on your phone: Android, **Install app**; iPhone, **Share > Add to Home Screen**.
- Turn on **Email me when we match** and **Nudge me at 48 hours** in Settings > Notifications.
- If an Introthread email lands in Spam, mark it **Not spam** once.
- Try everything first with **Explore the live demo account** on the home page.
