About Introthread

We started with a simple observation.

The most valuable part of networking happens after the introduction, and that’s exactly where it falls apart.

“

Card apps capture contacts. Graph tools guess relationships. Nobody records the moment two people actually meet and agree on what happens next.

Our story

Fixing relationship data
at the source.

Introthread was built by people who spent years keeping network data accurate for one of the world’s largest networks of networks. We saw where relationship data breaks: it is guessed from email traffic, scraped from profiles and never confirmed by the people involved.

So we started at the source, the moment two people meet. A Thread both people confirm, one honest question about what happens next, and a gentle nudge so it actually does.
