Our principles

Four promises,
built in.

Two-sided by design

A Thread only becomes verified when both people confirm it. Either person can delete their side at any time.

Private answers

Answers to What’s next? stay hidden until both people reply. A Not now is never shown to the other person.

Your notes are yours

Private context you add to a Thread is never visible to the other person, an organiser or a network.

Network visibility is opt-in

Warmth only includes Threads where both members opted in. Organisers see aggregate outcomes only.

Regulation

Designed for UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and applicable US state privacy laws. Export and deletion are built in. Questions about your data: privacy@introthread.com

Privacy policy

in plain English.

1. Who we are

Introthread (“we”, “us”) provides digital professional cards and shared Threads that record when two people meet. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us at privacy@introthread.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address and password (stored as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Card data: job title, company, city, bio, links, profile photo and card style you choose to add.
  • Thread data: who you met, when and where, your private notes and your answer to What’s next?.
  • Details of people you meet or import: when you add someone (typed, scanned from a QR code or business card, pasted, picked from your phone contacts or imported from a LinkedIn, Google or Outlook file), we store the name, email, phone, LinkedIn, job title, company and other details you choose to keep. Importing never contacts anyone.
  • Billing data: if you buy a paid plan, your plan, payment status and invoices. Card details are handled by our payment provider and never reach us.
  • Time and location of a Thread: when you save a Thread, the exact time and, if you allow it, your phone’s location. See “Location when you save a Thread” below.
  • Technical data: basic device and log information needed to keep the service secure.

3. Location when you save a Thread

When you save a new Thread, we record the exact time you saved it. If you allow it, we also record your phone’s location at that moment: the coordinates, how accurate they are (for example “about 12 m”) and a place name, so you can remember where you met.

  • Your permission: your phone asks before sharing your location. If you say no, the Thread is still saved, with the time only.
  • Only your own location: we record the location of your phone, never the location of the person you met.
  • Place name: to turn the coordinates into a place name, we send them to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service. We send only the coordinates, not your name or any details about the person you met.
  • Who can see it: the time and location are shown only to you, on your Thread, and are included when you export your data.
  • Turn it off: you can stop recording location at any time in Settings, under “Your data and privacy”, with the “Save where I meet people” switch. You can also remove location permission in your phone or browser settings.

We use this data to provide the feature you chose to use (contract), and the location part only with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time. It is kept and deleted with the Thread it belongs to.

4. Business card and QR scanning

When you scan a business card, the text is read on your own phone or computer. The photo is not uploaded to us; only the details you choose to save are stored with your Thread or contact.

5. AI follow-up drafts

If you switch on “Draft follow-ups with AI”, we send the context of that one Thread (names, where you met, your note and your answers) to our AI provider to write a suggested message. Drafts are never sent without you. Our AI provider processes this data only to create the draft, under a data processing agreement, and may not use it to train its models. You can switch this off at any time in Settings.

6. How and why we use it

We use your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), to keep it secure and improve it (legitimate interests), and to send service messages such as sign-in codes, Thread invitations to the person you met, and follow-up reminders you have switched on. When you start a Thread with someone, we email them once so they can confirm it; they can ignore it and nothing more is sent. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

7. Who can see what

Your public card is visible to anyone with your link. A Thread is only created when both people confirm it. Your answer to What’s next? stays hidden until both people reply, and your private notes are never shown to anyone else. Event organisers and networks only see aggregate results, and only where you opted in.

8. Service providers and transfers

We use trusted providers to run Introthread, including hosting (Vercel), database and authentication (Supabase), email delivery (Resend), payments (Paystack), AI follow-up drafts when you switch them on, sign-in with Google, Microsoft or LinkedIn when you choose it, fonts (Google Fonts) and place names for saved locations (OpenStreetMap Nominatim, which receives only coordinates). Where data is transferred outside the UK or EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your card and your side of every Thread within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records by law.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and applicable US state laws you can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time. You can export or delete your data in Settings, or email privacy@introthread.com. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

11. Cookies and storage

We only use storage that is strictly necessary to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add product analytics to understand how the service is used, we will ask for your consent first where the law requires it and update this policy.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is protected by row-level security so you can only read and change your own records, and passwords are never stored in plain text.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will tell you by email or in the app before they take effect.
